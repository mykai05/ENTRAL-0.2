## Working public brand

Recommended working brand: **RivetRelay**

Positioning: Practical operating kits for specialty contractors - from first inquiry through approved scope and final payment.

The name combines a trade-construction cue (`Rivet`) with the product line's handoff and follow-up function (`Relay`) without promising revenue, legal protection, automation, or a financial result. Exact-name web, trademark-index, Etsy, and social searches on 2026-08-03 found no obvious exact commercial use. That negative search is only a weak screening signal. It is not trademark, common-law, domain, social, or provider-handle clearance.

Owner approval and legal/provider availability review remain required before publication. `ScopeLedger Co.` is rejected: a current `ScopeLedger` contractor/change-order product at https://scopeledger.net/ directly overlaps the proposed market, and additional current ScopeLedger commercial uses were found at https://www.cvzp.com/ and https://techephi.com/. Other rejected working names include FieldLedger, ScopeCraft, TradeCommand, Contractor Command, TradeOwner, CrewSignal, JobSignal, TradeOps Toolkit, and FieldDesk Works because current businesses or applications already use the terms.

## Market evidence

- Jobber's 2026 Home Service Trends Report surveyed 1,050 United States home-service owners. It reports lead-response speed as important and identifies jobsite management, customer communication, quoting, paperwork, invoicing, and delayed payment as operating burdens. The supported product promise is better visibility and consistent next-action workflows, not a guaranteed financial result. Source: https://www.getjobber.com/home-service-trends-report/
- Associated General Contractors guidance describes change orders as formal changes to scope or contract and notes that approval and payment delays can interrupt schedules and cash flow. The supported product function is to record the requested change, cost and schedule effect, approval state, and billing linkage. It does not promise legal protection or payment. Source: https://www.agc.org/change-orders
- Etsy's contractor-template category contains thousands of low-cost isolated forms alongside higher-priced connected bundles and dashboards. The product line must differentiate through original connected workflows, instructions, examples, editable sources, tested delivery files, versioning, support, and licensing - not blank forms. Source: https://www.etsy.com/market/contractor_templates
- Etsy's invoice and payment category repeatedly uses objective terms such as invoice tracker, payment manager, outstanding balance, overdue, Excel, and Google Sheets. Source: https://www.etsy.com/market/invoice_tracker_and_payment
- Etsy's change-order category repeatedly uses contractor, change order, scope of work, approval, editable Word, and PDF. Source: https://www.etsy.com/market/change_order_forms
- Etsy's April 2026 title guidance recommends clear objective titles, generally under 15 words, and discourages repetition and keyword stuffing. Source: https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1399426136697

Observed Gumroad seller comparables place individual contractor workflow products near the owner's approved $29, $39, and $49 test prices. Seller listings are weak market evidence and are not outcome proof. Sources:

- Original seller-created digital downloads are allowed; copied bundles, unowned content, and generic prompt bundles are not.
- AI-assisted seller creations require disclosure in the listing description.
- Instant-download listings support at most five files, each no larger than 20 MB.
- Onboarding may require account creation, shop preferences and name, first listing, Etsy Payments, seller and tax classification, bank verification, billing card, a dynamic nonrefundable setup fee, government-ID and selfie verification, and two-factor authentication.
- The owner's first-listing and open-shop actions remain required because the first listing is part of onboarding.
- Etsy currently states a $0.20 listing fee, 6.5 percent transaction fee, and applicable Etsy Payments processing fees. The dynamic setup fee and taxes must be read back in the provider flow before spend.
- Five initial listing fees total $1.00. Dynamic setup fee, tax, and listing fees must remain at or below the owner-approved $150 maximum.
- Etsy Ads remain disabled. Offsite Ads must be read back and opted out where the provider permits; no paid-advertising budget is authorized.

## Conservative claims envelope

Allowed only when the files prove the statement: original, editable, tested for file integrity, includes named files and instructions, records, organizes, tracks, and surfaces. `No subscription` is allowed only if every delivered workflow is self-contained.

Forbidden without measured evidence or qualified review: more booked jobs, higher close rate, instant response, faster payment, protected profit or revenue, prevented disputes, legally binding, compliant, guaranteed ROI, accurate real-time KPIs, automatic, zero friction, works for every trade, replaces a CRM/accountant/attorney, time-savings claims, and fabricated testimonials or results.

The exact store, brand, products, prices, files, claims, license, refund and privacy terms, AI disclosure, support identity, and publication envelope require owner approval before the first external publication.
