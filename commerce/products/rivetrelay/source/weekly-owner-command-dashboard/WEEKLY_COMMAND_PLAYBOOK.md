# Weekly command playbook

## A. Data-close checklist

- Confirm week, timezone, and as-of time.
- Pull each source from its approved system.
- Reconcile leads and estimates to stable record IDs.
- Reconcile approved/completed/unbilled changes to the change register.
- Reconcile issued invoices and cash received to accounting/provider evidence.
- Reconcile total and past-due AR.
- Confirm active and at-risk project counts from project status records.
- Confirm next-week available and committed capacity use the same unit.
- Count open owner decisions from the decision queue.
- Count safety holds without copying sensitive details.
- Record all unavailable inputs.
- Create the evidence packet reference and lock the cutoff.

## B. Twenty-five-minute owner review

### Minutes 0-5 — control and blockers

Read `Overall_Status`, `Data_Quality_Flag`, safety holds, top exception, and open owner decisions. If data quality is `REVIEW`, decide whether the meeting can proceed for unaffected topics and assign reconciliation.

### Minutes 5-10 — demand and decisions

Review received/responded leads, issued/decided/accepted estimates, and the factual lead or estimate exceptions. Avoid interpreting a rate without cohort size and data quality.

### Minutes 10-15 — delivery and capacity

Review active projects, at-risk projects, approved change value, completed unbilled change value, and next-week capacity. Identify the exact constraint and its owner.

### Minutes 15-20 — billing and cash

Review invoices issued, cash actually received, open AR, past-due AR, and credits/refunds. Keep issued, earned, collected, and forecast values distinct.

### Minutes 20-25 — decisions and next actions

Resolve or route the highest-authority decisions. Confirm no more than three material next actions, each with one owner, due date, and evidence or task reference.

## E. Next-action test

A next action is acceptable when it:

- begins with an observable verb;
- names one accountable owner;
- has a due date or explicit trigger;
- links to the condition and evidence;
- stays within the person's authority;
- defines completion;
- states a hold or escalation route when authority/data is missing.

Weak: “Improve collections.”
Bounded: “Morgan will reconcile FICTIONAL-INV-705 ledger variance against the invoice and payment records by July 13; routine contact remains held until variance is zero or owner review is recorded.”

The example above is fictional.

## F. End-of-review readback

- Current status and its evidence basis.
- Data unavailable or under review.
- Top three exceptions.
- Decisions made and decisions still open.
- Three next actions, owners, and dates.
- Any stop condition requiring immediate escalation.
- Evidence packet and meeting record references.

## G. Monthly trend review

Use closed weekly rows to observe patterns, but do not imply causation. Check whether definitions or source boundaries changed before comparing rates. Investigate unusually favorable results with the same diligence as unfavorable results.
