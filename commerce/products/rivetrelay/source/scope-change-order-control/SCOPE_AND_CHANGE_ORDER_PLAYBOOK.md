## C. Change proposal content checklist

- Stable change-order ID and revision.
- Reference to current agreement/project.
- Existing condition or request.
- Exact added, removed, or revised scope.
- Explicit exclusions and assumptions.
- Price impact with currency and tax treatment.
- Schedule impact or `not yet determined`.
- Effect on prior allowances or selections.
- Expiration or decision deadline only if approved locally.
- Decision options and approved acceptance method.
- Authorized issuer identity.
- Attachments/evidence list.
- Statement that changed work is not scheduled solely by issuing the proposal, if consistent with the contract.

## E. Communication scripts

### Condition identified; evaluation pending

Subject: Project [project reference] — condition under review

Hello [customer name],

During [verified activity], the team observed [factual condition]. We are comparing it with the current approved scope before recommending a next step. No added permanent work is authorized by this notice.

We expect to provide [an evaluation/a proposed change/no-change confirmation] by [supported date or window]. If you have a relevant document or decision-authority update, please send it through [approved route].

[Authorized sender]

### Change proposal ready for review

Subject: Proposed change [change-order ID and revision] for [project reference]

Hello [customer name],

Proposed change [ID/revision] documents [verified scope summary], a price impact of [approved amount and tax statement], and a schedule impact of [approved impact or not yet determined]. Please review the attached scope, assumptions, exclusions, and decision method.

The team will treat the proposal according to the current agreement and will not infer approval from silence.

[Authorized sender]

### Approved change internal handoff

Change [ID/revision] has a recorded approval reference at [secure reference]. Operations may schedule only the approved scope and revision. Amount: [approved amount]. Schedule impact: [approved impact]. Billing status: [status]. Open conditions: [verified conditions or none].

### Declined or expired change internal handoff

Change [ID/revision] is [declined/expired] as of [date] with evidence at [secure reference]. Remove the proposed changed work from the active schedule unless another approved obligation applies. Preserve base-scope work and escalate any ambiguity.

## F. Pre-work checklist

- Correct change ID and revision.
- Current scope comparison completed.
- Approval state verified from evidence.
- Approved amount and schedule impact match the proposal.
- Crew has the approved scope, not an obsolete draft.
- Required materials and permits confirmed.
- Safety and access conditions reviewed.
- Billing handoff owner identified.

## G. Completion-to-billing checklist

- Performed work matches the approved revision.
- Completion evidence is stored.
- Deviations are separately documented.
- Approved amount reconciles to billing instruction.
- Invoice reference is recorded after actual issuance.
- Credits, disputes, or partial completion are visible.
- Customer-facing statement uses actual invoice and contract facts.
