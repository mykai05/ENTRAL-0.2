# Response and follow-up playbook

These scripts are starting language, not automated messages. Confirm the recipient, event, channel eligibility, job facts, and sender identity before use. Replace bracketed fields with verified information; if a fact is not known, omit it.

## Script 1 — first response after an inquiry

Subject: Your [project type] inquiry with [business name]

Hello [customer name],

Thank you for contacting [business name] about [brief verified project description]. I’m [sender name], and I’ll coordinate the next step. To route this correctly, could you confirm [one or two specific facts needed for qualification]?

Our next step is [verified next step]. I can [call/reply] on [specific available window]. If another channel or time works better, let me know.

[Sender name] / [Business name] / [Approved contact information]

## Script 2 — voicemail after a permitted call attempt

“Hello [customer name], this is [sender name] with [business name] returning your inquiry about [project type]. I’m calling to confirm [specific next step]. You can reach us at [approved number] during [business hours], or reply through [approved alternate channel]. Again, this is [sender name] with [business name].”

Record the attempt once. Do not repeatedly call to satisfy an internal count.

## Script 3 — estimate delivery

Subject: [business name] estimate [estimate reference]

Hello [customer name],

Attached or linked is estimate [estimate reference] for [verified scope summary]. Please review the included scope, assumptions, exclusions, price, and timing. The estimate shows [expiration or review date only if present in the approved estimate].

I can walk through the document on [available window]. Questions or requested changes will be documented before any revised estimate or agreement is issued.

[Sender name]

## Script 4 — estimate follow-up

Subject: Question about estimate [estimate reference]

Hello [customer name],

I’m following up on estimate [estimate reference], delivered [verified date]. Do you have a question about the scope, timing, or next step that I can route to the right person?

No action has been scheduled from this message alone. If you want to proceed, pause, or close the request, reply with your preference and we will document it.

[Sender name]

## Script 5 — documented requested callback

Subject: Confirming the callback you requested

Hello [customer name],

This confirms your request for a callback about [topic] on [date or agreed window]. [Owner name] is assigned. If the timing changes, reply here or use [approved contact route].

[Sender name]

## Script 6 — close-the-loop message

Subject: Closing estimate follow-up for [estimate reference]

Hello [customer name],

We have not recorded a decision on estimate [estimate reference]. Unless you ask us to keep it open, we will close the follow-up record on [date]. Closing the follow-up does not create a cancellation fee or change signed obligations; any existing agreement remains governed by its own terms.

If you would like the team to review a change or next step, reply through [approved channel].

[Sender name]

## New-inquiry checklist

- Confirm the inquiry belongs to the business.
- Assign a unique internal lead ID.
- Record received time and source from evidence.
- Check contact eligibility and channel preference.
- Capture only the minimum qualification facts needed.
- Assign one owner.
- Set the next action and date.
- Send or record only an eligible first response.

## Estimate-delivery checklist

- Confirm the estimate is approved for delivery.
- Confirm the recipient and delivery channel.
- Verify the scope reference, amount, and revision.
- Store the estimate in the approved location.
- Record delivery date and secure evidence reference.
- Set the next action; do not assume customer acceptance.

## Follow-up checklist

- Verify the estimate was actually delivered.
- Review the latest customer preference or opt-out.
- Check whether another team member already contacted the customer.
- Use a script appropriate to the current event.
- Record the attempt once, including channel and date.
- Record the actual response or `NO_RESPONSE`; do not infer sentiment.
- Set the next action or close with a supported outcome.

## Weekly exception checklist

- Open lead with no owner.
- Open lead with no next action.
- Delivered estimate missing a delivery reference.
- Follow-up date in the past.
- Unknown contact eligibility.
- More than one open record for the same underlying inquiry.
- Closed record without outcome evidence.
