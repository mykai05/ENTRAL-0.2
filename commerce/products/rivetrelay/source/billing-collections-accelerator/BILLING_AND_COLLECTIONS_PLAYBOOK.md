# Billing and collections playbook

Every external message below requires verified facts, recipient, channel, authority, and applicable rules. Replace bracketed text only with supported values. Do not add threats, legal consequences, interest, penalties, or collection fees unless separately authorized and verified.

## Invoice issuance checklist

- Accounting invoice is actually issued.
- Invoice ID, issue date, due date, and amount match the ledger.
- Customer/project reference is correct.
- Scope and approved changes reconcile.
- Credits, deposits, tax, and prior payments reconcile.
- Supporting documents are included where required.
- Recipient and delivery channel are verified.
- Delivery evidence reference is stored.
- Next action and date are assigned.

## Script 1 — invoice delivered

Subject: Invoice [invoice ID] from [business name]

Hello [recipient name],

Invoice [invoice ID] was issued on [issue date] for [verified project or milestone description]. The invoice total is [actual amount] and the stated due date is [due date]. The invoice and supporting documents are available at [approved secure delivery route].

If the bill-to information, payment allocation, or supporting record needs review, contact [approved route] and reference the invoice ID.

[Authorized sender]

## Script 2 — courtesy reminder before or on due date

Subject: Invoice [invoice ID] — due [due date]

Hello [recipient name],

This is a reminder that invoice [invoice ID] shows an open balance of [verified ledger balance] with a stated due date of [due date]. If payment has already been initiated, no additional payment action is requested from this reminder; please share the remittance reference through [approved route] so the team can reconcile it.

Questions or disputes can be sent to [approved route].

[Authorized sender]

## Script 3 — past-due follow-up

Subject: Account review for invoice [invoice ID]

Hello [recipient name],

As of [verified as-of date], invoice [invoice ID] shows an open balance of [verified ledger balance] and a stated due date of [due date]. Please confirm one of the following through [approved route]: payment information for reconciliation, a specific question or dispute, or the date you expect to provide an update.

This message does not add a fee or change the underlying agreement.

[Authorized sender]

## Script 4 — payment promise confirmation

Subject: Confirmation of your update for invoice [invoice ID]

Hello [recipient name],

This confirms the update received on [date]: [promised amount] expected by [promised date], subject to actual receipt and reconciliation. The invoice remains open until the accounting record confirms payment. If any detail is incorrect, reply through [approved route].

[Authorized sender]

## Script 5 — dispute acknowledgment

Subject: Review opened for invoice [invoice ID]

Hello [recipient name],

We recorded your question about [factual dispute topic] on [date]. Routine follow-up on the disputed item is paused while [assigned owner] reviews [specific records]. We expect to provide a status update by [supported date or window].

This acknowledgment does not decide the dispute. Please send additional relevant records through [approved secure route].

[Authorized sender]

## Script 6 — reconciled resolution

Subject: Resolution record for invoice [invoice ID]

Hello [recipient name],

The review of [factual topic] is complete. The current accounting record shows [verified resolution: balance/credit/payment allocation/corrected invoice reference]. Supporting documentation is available at [approved route]. If this does not match your records, contact [approved dispute route] and reference [resolution reference].

[Authorized sender]

## Weekly reconciliation checklist

- Open-invoice total matches the authoritative report.
- Every payment/credit imported has an evidence reference.
- No paid invoice remains in an active follow-up queue.
- Ledger variances are held.
- Disputed invoices use the dispute route.
- Promise dates are reviewed without recording promises as cash.
- Service holds have explicit authority and contract review.
- Refunds, chargebacks, and write-off reviews are separately visible.
- Owner decisions are documented.
